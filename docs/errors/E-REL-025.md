# E-REL-025: sign in again to approve

An approval releases an app that *Needs approval*, so the platform asks for a fresh sign-in with
the company login: you must have signed in within the last five minutes, by the platform's clock.
You signed in to the console longer ago. Nothing was released, and the card still waits. When you
sign in again, use the same account: an approval from another account is refused.

**Fix:** sign in again to approve
