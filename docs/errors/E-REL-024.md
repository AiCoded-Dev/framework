# E-REL-024: you built this publish

You tried to approve an approval card for a publish that you built. The person who asks for a
release never approves it, even when they are one of the app type's approvers or a security admin:
the approval is a second person's look at what the app may now do. You can see the card, but
neither approve nor decline it. Nothing was released, and the card still waits.

**Fix:** another approver of the app type must approve it
