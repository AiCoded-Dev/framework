# E-MIG-001: the draft cannot be used

The next schema change of an app is its draft, `.aicoded/migration.sql`: SQL statements, each ended by `;`. A draft holds at most 64 KiB of UTF-8 text and at most 100 statements, and at least one statement besides comments. It is a plain file in the app's folder: `aicoded check` refuses a folder or another special file there, a link that leads out of the app's folder, and a draft it cannot read. The draft travels with a publish, not in git: `aicoded publish` refuses a commit that holds `.aicoded/migration.sql`. It goes with one publish only: while the publish that carried it has not ended, `aicoded publish` and the `publish` tool refuse to send it again, since its statements would then reach the app's migrations twice; once that publish passed, the draft is deleted.

**Fix:** keep the draft to 64 KiB of UTF-8 and 100 statements, split a larger change over two publishes, delete a draft with no statement, untrack a committed draft with `git rm --cached .aicoded/migration.sql`, and wait for a publish that carried the draft to end, as `aicoded status <id>` or the `release_status` tool shows, before you publish again
