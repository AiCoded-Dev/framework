# E-PUB-005: another builder's app

The first publish of an app's name in your organisation creates the app, owned by the builder who published it, and only its owner can publish it again or see its publishes. An app of this name exists in your organisation and is not yours. Before `aicoded` says so, it refreshes your sign-in once, which brings the apps you own now, such as one that an administrator has just transferred to you.

**Fix:** publish under a name of your own: change `app:` in `aicoded.yaml`, or ask your administrator to transfer the app to you.
