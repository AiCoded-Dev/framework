# E-REL-026: the approval card changed

An approval names what the card showed: the permission list, by its hash, the built app, by its
digest, and the decision, with its app type and reasons. The platform decides again when you
approve, and what it decides now is not what you saw: a newer publish of the app arrived, the
permission list or the built app differs, the app type or its group map changed so that the
reasons differ, or the card no longer waits because it was approved or declined meanwhile.
Rather than release something you did not see, the platform refuses the approval. Nothing was
released by it.

**Fix:** open the card again
