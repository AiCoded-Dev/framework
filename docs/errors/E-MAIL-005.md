# E-MAIL-005: message too large or too many recipients or attachments

One message goes to at most 50 recipients (To, Cc and Bcc together), carries at most 20 attachments, and is at most 10 MiB in total, counting the subject, the bodies, and the attachments with their content types.

**Fix:** send to fewer people at once, or save large files in a file store and link to them.
