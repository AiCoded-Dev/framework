# E-PUB-013: unknown publish

`aicoded status`, and the `release_status` tool of `aicoded mcp`, show a publish of one of your apps by its id. The platform knows no publish with this id among them: the id is mistyped, or it is a publish of another organisation or of an app you do not own.

**Fix:** copy the id from what `aicoded publish` or the `publish` tool printed.
