# E-PUB-013: unknown publish

`aicoded status`, and the `release_status` tool of `aicoded mcp`, show a publish of one of your apps by its id. The platform knows no publish with this id in your organisation: the id is mistyped, or it is a publish of another organisation. A publish of an app of your organisation that you do not own is E-PUB-005 instead.

**Fix:** copy the id from what `aicoded publish` or the `publish` tool printed.
